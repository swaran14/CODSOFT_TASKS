Difficulty

"Unbeatable" runs a full minimax(depth, alpha, beta) search of the game tree — the AI literally cannot lose from an empty board. The other modes inject randomness to give you a fighting chance.

How it works

Every empty cell is tried recursively. Each branch is scored +10 for an AI win, -10 for a human win, 0 for a draw (adjusted by depth so the AI prefers faster wins). Alpha-beta pruning cuts branches that can't affect the final decision, so it runs instantly even at full search depth.
